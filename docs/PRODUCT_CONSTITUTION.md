# VoltFlow Product Constitution

VoltFlow is the operating system for electrical contractors.

Every interaction should save time, reduce mistakes, improve clarity, or increase professionalism.

## Core principles

1. Connected workflows, not isolated pages.
2. Office staff and technicians should not enter the same information twice.
3. AI assists; humans remain responsible for approval.
4. Company-specific behavior should be configurable.
5. Motion must communicate state and never slow the user.
6. Customer-visible content must be intentionally approved.
7. Security, auditability, and data isolation are product requirements.
